# 1.2 | 10 Nov 2026
+ Liquid Glass: a new look for the hotbar's hearts, hunger and armor - clear glass that only takes a tint of the colour (Hotbar > Icon Look)
* Hotbar: hearts, hunger and armor are back in Minecraft's layout, with smooth glass icons that stay sharp at any GUI scale
* Panels layout: click a category's title to fold it in (drag it to move it, like before)
* Radar: clicking it opens a window in the middle of the screen instead of the whole screen
* More FPS: every HUD card is kept and repeated on its own, so one card with items no longer makes all of them draw every frame
* Clean Style: liquid glass everywhere - a glossy shine, a bright edge on top and light along the bottom on windows, menus, pop-ups and the hotbar
* Liquid Glass icons: more depth - a second reflection and a darker band under the shine
* Panels layout: folding and unfolding a category slides smoothly
* What's New: shows once after every update, as soon as the title screen is up
- Profiles: any profile can be deleted (also the one in use), new profiles start from the default settings, and servers linked to a profile switch every time you join

# 1.1 | 10 Nov 2026
+ Setting Profiles: keep different setups (PvP, Building, Recording...) and switch in one click - or link a server so joining it switches for you
+ Share & Backup: copy all your settings as one code to send to friends, or use theirs - your old settings are always kept to undo
+ Custom Hotbar: the hotbar, hearts and hunger drawn in your style, with a smooth sliding selection
+ Style Editor: tune roundness, see-through-ness, borders, shadows, glow and the glass shine with a live preview
+ Menu Button Look: pick rounded, glass or Minecraft buttons for the game's menus, whatever style you use
+ What's New: this window - it shows once after every update
* Pop-ups: follow the style you picked - square in Vanilla Style, frosted glass in Clean Style, calmer in the normal look
* Crosshair: live preview in its settings
* Chat: one time stamp per message instead of one per line
- Account button: long names no longer overlap
- Panels layout: modules you opened stay open when you come back
